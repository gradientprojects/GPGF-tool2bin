GPGF-tool2bin

Photo of a tool → Gridfinity bin STEP. Everything runs in your browser — photos never leave your device.

1 · Grab a sheet of paper (template optional)

Any plain letter/A4 sheet works as the backdrop — no printing needed. The printed template below is worth it when the quick way struggles: uneven light or shadows ("paper sheet not found"), a white or light table the sheet blends into, or when you want the best accuracy — its marker border corrects perspective far better than four paper corners, and its 100 mm ruler lets you verify the scale. Print at 100%, place the tool in the gray field.

🖨 Letter template  ·  🖨 A4 template

2 · Scan a tool

Tool on the sheet. On a phone this opens the camera. Or drag a photo anywhere on the page. (sets the scale; ignored when the template is detected).

Photo tips: shoot straight down over the tool. For thick tools, step back and use 2×/3× optical zoom if your phone has it — up close, the camera sees the tool's sides and the outline comes out slightly oversized (sheet correction can't fix that). Even, diffuse light beats flash: flash puts glare on shiny tools, but your camera's own flash toggle is there if it's too dim.
